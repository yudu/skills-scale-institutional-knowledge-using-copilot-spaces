# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads design and execute test strategies to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards. They collaborate with developers and product managers to validate solutions and identify defects early.

### Responsibilities
- Own test strategy and test plan for the project
- Define acceptance testing criteria and protocols
- Execute manual and automated testing across environments
- Triage and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Validate that all acceptance criteria are met before sign-off
- Mentor the team on testing best practices

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Enable fast, confident releases
- Maintain quality standards and customer satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and test case walkthroughs
- Defect reports and quality metrics in status updates
- Participation in acceptance and release reviews

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, ensure architectural consistency, and guide engineering decisions. They partner with developers to solve complex problems and manage technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Conduct code reviews and maintain code quality standards
- Mentor developers and drive technical growth
- Ensure alignment with platform standards and infrastructure

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Foster a culture of engineering excellence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and guidance
- Technical risk escalation and mitigation planning

---

## Scrum Master/Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Delivery Facilitators enable the team to execute effectively by facilitating agile ceremonies, removing impediments, and protecting team capacity. They partner closely with Project Managers and developers to maintain sustainable delivery pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Protect the team from scope creep and external interruptions
- Maintain sprint cadence and team velocity metrics
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track sprint progress and escalate blockers to Project Manager

### Goals
- Enable consistent, predictable delivery
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Foster a culture of transparency and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and blocker tracking
- Sprint retrospectives and action item follow-up
- Velocity and capacity planning discussions
- Escalation of blockers to Project Manager

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors champion the project, provide business context and strategic alignment, and make critical prioritization decisions. They ensure the project delivers value to the broader organization and remove business-level constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Champion the project and secure executive sponsorship
- Provide business context and strategic priorities
- Make go/no-go decisions and resource allocation choices
- Unblock business-level constraints and approvals
- Provide feedback on progress and course corrections
- Communicate project value to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational goals
- Enable fast decision-making and risk mitigation at leadership level

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and status briefings
- Strategic alignment reviews and priority trade-offs
- Executive decision-making on scope, timeline, and resources
- Risk escalation and mitigation planning

---

## Operations/Infrastructure Owner

### Role Summary
Operations and Infrastructure Owners manage deployment environments, infrastructure concerns, observability, and production support readiness. They ensure the platform can reliably host and scale the delivered features.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment environments (staging, production)
- Ensure infrastructure scalability, reliability, and security
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting for production systems
- Plan and execute deployment procedures and rollbacks
- Conduct capacity planning and performance tuning
- Support incident response and post-incident analysis

### Goals
- Ensure high availability and performance in production
- Enable fast, safe deployments with minimal risk
- Maintain observability and quick incident resolution

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and coordination before releases
- Infrastructure requirements and capacity planning reviews
- Production incident alerts and post-mortems
- Performance metrics and system health dashboards
